Mosaic Team Handbook

Annual leave requests should be submitted through the employee portal at least five working days before the planned leave date. Employees receive twenty annual leave days per calendar year. Their manager approves requests within two business days.

Remote work is available up to three days each week, subject to manager approval. Team members should update their shared calendar with remote-work days.

New team members complete onboarding during their first two weeks. The onboarding checklist includes security training, access setup, a product walkthrough, and a meeting with their manager.
